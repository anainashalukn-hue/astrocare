How spaceflight affects your body

Daily preventive reminders

Exercise
Complete your mission-approved resistance and cardio sessions to protect bones and muscles.
Sleep
Keep a regular sleep schedule and dim lights before bed.
Hydration
Drink fluids regularly. Thirst signals are weaker in microgravity.
Nutrition
Eat all planned meals, with enough protein, calcium and vitamin D.
Crew wellbeing
Talk with crewmates, keep a routine, and contact family and support teams.
Please note: ASTROCARE is an educational prototype made for the NASA Space Apps Challenge. It uses simple illustrative rules, not medical thresholds, and is not a medical device. It does not diagnose anything. In a real mission, always follow your flight surgeon's guidance. Nothing you enter leaves your browser.

Future versions: secure data storage, wearable sensors, personal baselines and mission-approved alert limits.
